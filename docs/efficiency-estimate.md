# Efficiency estimate and measurement policy

This project treats efficiency estimates as hypotheses until A/B tested.

## Current engineering estimate

For a complete package combining DME and later EGS work:

- **whole-trip development target:** roughly 12-20% fuel-use reduction in favourable use;
- **ambitious steady-cruise experiment:** potentially around 20-25% under highly favourable conditions;
- **1:17 from a 1:11.5 baseline:** requires about 32% reduction and is therefore a stretch target, not a forecast.

The individual feature estimates must **not** be added together because they overlap.

## Why firing-density gain is uncertain

The N62 has Valvetronic, already reducing throttling losses, and does not mechanically close the valves of unfired cylinders. Therefore 4/8 or 6/8 firing may improve active-cylinder load/BSFC but cannot recover all pumping/friction losses like a purpose-built cylinder-deactivation engine.

## Validation rule

Every efficiency claim should be based on repeated same-route tests with comparable:

- ambient conditions;
- fuel;
- tyre pressure;
- vehicle mass;
- speed profile;
- warm-up state;
- traffic exposure.

Use fuel-flow/injection data and tank-to-tank measurements where practical; do not rely on the dashboard average alone.
